# mod.io listing

## Name

Kroky's No Pain, All Gain

## Summary

Turn harmful Agility modifiers into equal-strength bonuses.

## Description

Reverses harmful Agility obstacle and pillar modifiers: -20% XP becomes +20% XP, and +20% resource costs becomes -20%.
Existing bonuses stay unchanged.

Supports normal and Abyssal Agility.
All expansions are optional.
No configuration or other mods required.

## Version

1.0.0

## Changelog

- Reverse harmful Agility obstacle and pillar modifiers while retaining existing bonuses.
- Support normal and Abyssal courses with all expansions optional.

## Upload

Upload **no-pain-all-gain-1.0.0.zip** with `manifest.json` and `setup.mjs` at the root and `assets/icon.png` inside `assets`.
The square mod icon is `assets/icon.png`.
The mod.io listing cover is `assets/cover.png`.
No mod dependency is required.
